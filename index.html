01 / THE PROBLEM

You control the AI you bought.
Not the AI your suppliers added.

For every supplier, you should be able to answer:

  1. Does this product use AI, and which features?
  2. Is it on by default, or did someone opt in?
  3. Which model provider sits behind it?
  4. What of our data reaches the model?
  5. Where is it processed, and for how long is it kept?
  6. Is it used to train models? Have we opted out?
  7. What changed since we last looked?

        04 / SECURITY

        Built by a security firm.
        Built to be attacked.

        A register of which firms use which suppliers is a target. We treat it like one, from the first line of code.

        Designed for launch

        Passkeys, not passwords

        Sign-in is passkey-first (WebAuthn / FIDO2): phishing-resistant, nothing reusable to steal. Hardware security keys supported. Team accounts can enforce SSO with Microsoft Entra ID or Google, with MFA required.

        Designed for launch

        Your keys, your tenant

        Every organisation gets its own data encryption keys, wrapped by a root key held in a UK-region HSM. Tenant isolation is enforced in the database, not just the app. Keys rotate on schedule, and leaving means crypto-shredding your data.

        Designed for launch

        Signed licences and updates

        Licences are signed tokens, verified against a published public key. Every release is signed, ships with a software bill of materials, and is verified before it runs. No silent changes.

        Designed for launch

        Tamper-evident audit trail

        Every sign-in, export, and change to a finding is written to a hash-chained audit log you can export to your SIEM. If anyone edits history, it shows.

        Designed for launch

        UK hosted, minimal data

        Hosted in the UK. We hold supplier metadata and your tags, not your documents or systems. We never scan your network, and no AI verdict reaches you without human review.

        Roadmap

        Independently tested

        Cyber Essentials Plus at launch, an independent penetration test before general availability, and ISO 27001 within 18 months. Reports available under NDA.
